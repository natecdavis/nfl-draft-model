Draft Simulator

Replay a team's draft at the picks it actually made and choose your own player each time. Other teams draft exactly as they really did. Values are the change in expected Super Bowls over five seasons, in % of one title, for that team's situation on draft night.

How it works: at each pick, everyone really taken before it is gone (including this team's own earlier picks, even if you passed on them), and anyone you took is gone at your later picks. Each player you take at a position lowers the value of the next one there; after one QB the QB need is filled, and a flagged QB need counts with its measured chance of being real. Candidates are Arif Hasan's consensus board plus drafted players who weren't on it (valued as ranked just past it, #330). Positions are the consensus board's own labels; a label that often changes position in the NFL (a 5-technique, an edge rusher who becomes an off-ball linebacker, a safety who moves to linebacker) is valued as a blend of those positions and shown like "DL/EDGE". Kickers and punters aren't valued. Draft-night trades aren't simulated. "Delivered" columns count each player's first four seasons; drafts since 2023 count the seasons played so far (through 2025). Undrafted players count from the 2016 class.
